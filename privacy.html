Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy describes the intended data handling of Paraclipper, a locally operated tool for authorized Twitch highlight creation and optional publishing to connected platforms. This policy must be reviewed and updated by the app operator before public launch.

Information processed

Paraclipper may process Twitch channel names, stream and clip metadata, video/audio files, publishing settings, and publishing history. When you connect a platform account, the app processes authorization tokens and account information needed to enable the requested features.

How information is used

Information is used to monitor authorized streams, detect candidate highlights, generate or prepare clips, and carry out publishing actions you enable. Paraclipper does not need your TikTok or Google password; supported connections use the platforms' authorization flows.

Storage and sharing

In the current local version, clip files, publishing history, and authorization tokens may be stored on the computer running Paraclipper. Information is transmitted to Twitch, TikTok, Google/YouTube, or their service providers when you use their connected features. Publishing a video may make it visible according to your selected privacy settings and platform rules.

Retention and security

Local data remains until removed by the operator or the relevant application cleanup process. Protect the computer and do not share API secrets or token files. No method of storage is perfectly secure.

Your choices

You can stop using the tool, remove locally stored data, and revoke platform access through the applicable platform account settings. Revoking access may not delete videos already published to a platform.

Children

Paraclipper is not intended for children under the age required by the relevant platforms.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as Paraclipper develops. For privacy questions, contact the operator through the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not post passwords, tokens, or sensitive personal information in public issues.
